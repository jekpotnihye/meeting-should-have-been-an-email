# The Meeting That Should Have Been an Email

One line, at most 200 characters, that makes a meeting that should
have been an email funny.

Entry (52 characters, no trailing newline):

> Nine of us met to discuss the email nobody had read.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the entry verbatim to `/output/entry.txt`
(`OUTPUT_PATH` overrides the destination for local checks).
It needs no arguments, no network, and no dependencies beyond
Python 3's standard library.

## Why this line

It names the meeting and the email in the first half, so the
subject is unmistakable, then lands its turn in the last word:
nine people gathered to discuss something nobody had read.
The angle is not how long the meeting ran or that it could
have been an email — it is that the email was never read at
all. It stands alone, needs no team or office context, and
every word earns its place.
